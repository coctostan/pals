<objective>

## Goal

Execute the retroactive harvest across the two external field deployments, verify and close any dialect gaps the foreign corpora expose, then specify and generate the aggregate cross-deployment roll-up that Phase 308 consumes as its proposal-batch evidence base.

## Purpose

Plan 307-01 built the normalization layer and proved it on this repository, validating it against the forward UNIFY ledger (19/19 exact on `306-01`). It deliberately deferred external execution and the aggregate roll-up. Phase 308 cannot author a source-backed proposal batch from a single deployment's evidence — the whole point of field harvest is cross-deployment signal.

This plan closes Phase 307 by producing that evidence base. It is the last plan of the phase.

## Output

- `.paul/field-harvest/quark-MODULE-LEDGER.md` and `.paul/field-harvest/hybrid-energy-reasoner-MODULE-LEDGER.md` — external harvest artifacts.
- `.paul/field-harvest/UNPARSEABLE.md` — extended to all three deployments via the harvester's existing merge behavior.

- `tools/rollup-field-harvest.sh` — deterministic roll-up generator.
- `.paul/field-harvest/HARVEST-ROLLUP.md` — the aggregate artifact.
- Extended fixtures, guardrails, and bounded validation sections.

## Field Reconnaissance (source-backed, gathered during PLAN via `--dry-run`, no writes)

Both deployment roots are present and readable. Dry-run harvest yields:

| Deployment | SUMMARYs | Rows | Manifest | Dominant reason code |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| quark | 154 | 73 | 121 | `no-dispatch-evidence` 107 |
| hybrid-energy-reasoner | 62 | 72 | 21 | `unmapped-status` 17 |
| pals (already harvested, 307-01) | 319 | 207 | 270 | `no-dispatch-evidence` 151 |

Remaining foreign manifest detail: quark `unmapped-status` 12, `unknown-module` 2; hybrid `no-dispatch-evidence` 3, `ambiguous-module-set` 1.

Status distributions differ materially from pals and from each other, which is itself the signal Phase 308 needs:

| Deployment | PASS | PASS_WITH_CONCERNS | WARN | BLOCK | SKIP | NOTE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| quark | 37 | 4 | 0 | 1 | 13 | 18 |
| hybrid-energy-reasoner | 37 | 0 | 5 | 0 | 21 | 9 |
| pals | 48 | 4 | 1 | 0 | 79 | 75 |

Notable: quark produces the corpus's **first and only `BLOCK`** row, and hybrid is the only deployment with a meaningful `WARN` population. pals is heavily `SKIP`/`NOTE` — consistent with a repo that dispatches all 20 modules against mostly out-of-scope surfaces.

**The central open question this plan must answer, not assume:** quark's 107 `no-dispatch-evidence` entries are 69% of its corpus, versus 47% for pals. A bounded spot-check of three (`01-kernel-core/01-01`, `01-kernel-core/01-02`, `01-minimum-viable-kernel/01-01`) confirmed zero occurrences of `Module Execution Reports`, `[dispatch]`, or `| Module |` — genuinely evidence-free early phases, matching the pals pattern. Three is not a proof over 107. Task 1 must verify this systematically before the roll-up reports it as a finding.

## Decisions carried into this plan

- **D1** — Harvest is read-only over external roots; no PALS artifact, config, or file is written into quark or hybrid-energy-reasoner. Enforced by the harvester's existing three-tier write boundary, which will be negative-tested again.
- **D2** — The roll-up is generated by a deterministic script, not hand-assembled, matching the 307-01 precedent that derived artifacts must be regenerable and diff-reviewable.
- **D3** — The roll-up reports recorded dispatch evidence, never module value. Contract §9 already forbids that claim; §10 must restate it at the aggregate level, where the temptation to rank modules is strongest.
